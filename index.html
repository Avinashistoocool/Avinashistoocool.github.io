01 / about me

I don't trust what I can't rebuild.

I'm Avinash, a physics student at IIT Delhi. I fell for physics because of one feeling: the moment an idea stops being something you memorised and becomes something that simply has to be true. Most of what I do is chasing that moment.

I believe understanding is earned, not borrowed. If I can't rebuild an idea from the first equation, I don't really know it yet. I'd rather be slow and right than fast and vague, and I'd rather say "I don't know yet" than pretend. Every result has limits, and I'd rather name them myself than let hype hide them.

I also believe knowledge should be open. Good ideas shouldn't sit behind paywalls, jargon or the right connections. If something took me weeks to understand, I want the next curious student to get there in an afternoon. Explaining an idea clearly to a beginner matters more to me than impressing an expert.
